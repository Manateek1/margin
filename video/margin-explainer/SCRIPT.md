# Margin — Narration Script (SCRIPT.md)

## Beat 1 — Hook & Overview
Splitting bills with roommates usually means awkward texts and rigid fifty-fifty math. Meet Margin: the privacy-first home planner that keeps roommates in sync.

## Beat 2 — Fair Available-Margin Split
Instead of a flat fifty-fifty split that ignores different incomes and personal essentials, Margin calculates each roommate's true available margin, so everyone contributes the same fair effort.

## Beat 3 — Monthly Plan & What-If Scenarios
Track recurring bills by due date, mark month-specific paid status, and export to CSV, or test a one-twenty dollar what-if expense side by side without touching your saved plan.

## Beat 4 — Zero-PII AI Conversation Coach
Need to bring up a bill change? Margin's AI coach runs Google Gemma 4 on Cloudflare Workers AI. Only your chosen goal and tone go to the cloud, while real names and dollar amounts are added strictly on your device.

## Beat 5 — Local AES-256-GCM Password Vault
And with Margin's local password vault, your plan is encrypted in the browser with AES-256-GCM and locks automatically on every reload.

## Beat 6 — Outro & Call to Action
Margin. Elevate the ordinary kitchen-table budget. Try it live at getmargin.pages.dev.
